# proam-labs-demos
Interactive product and architecture demos from proAM Labs.

## What if your AI didn't make you start over?

Today, conversations, research, decisions, and project history are scattered across applications. Every interruption, handoff, or change of tools can force us to reconstruct what we already understood.

proAM Labs is exploring a different approach: **preserving qualified understanding across time, tools, and interactions**, while keeping evidence, uncertainty, and human authority intact.

These interactive prototypes show how that approach is being developed—and what it may eventually make possible. They are demonstrations of specific capabilities, **not a claim that the full system is already operating in production**.

## Resume-to-Submission — RTS Checkpoint Pilot

- [v0.3 — Opportunity Presented → Resume-to-Submission (current pilot)](https://jcholman.github.io/proam-labs-demos/rts-checkpoint-pilot-v0-3/) — Start the clock when the **job description is first presented**, not when the resume is presented. Eight user-attested milestones include qualification, alignment, resume creation and verification, explicit Ready-to-Submit approval, submission receipt, and outcome. Track **JD → Ready**, **JD → Submission**, and **Resume → Ready** wall-clock intervals alongside optional active effort and self-estimated saved time. Lightweight trust/continuity feedback, HOLD and correction, defer/decline, local history and JSON/CSV export. This is a demo, not an ATS or the canonical RTS tracker. [Protocol and limits](./rts-checkpoint-pilot-v0-3/README.md).

- [v0.2 — Previous timed checkpoint demo (preserved)](https://jcholman.github.io/proam-labs-demos/rts-checkpoint-pilot-v0-2/) — Track **actual begin and end timestamps for each RTS checkpoint**, including a distinct **Resume Presented → Ready to Submit** wall-clock interval (10 minutes or 10 days). Each accepted milestone closes its interval and starts the next; a HOLD preserves the elapsed clock without advancing. Optional historical timestamps are provenance-labeled. Active work minutes and self-estimated time saved remain separate, with the same two lightweight trust/continuity questions and local JSON/CSV exports. [Timing contract, limitations and test cases](./rts-checkpoint-pilot-v0-2/README.md).

- [v0.1 — Previous checkpoint-only demo (preserved)](https://jcholman.github.io/proam-labs-demos/rts-checkpoint-pilot-v0-1/) — Follow a qualified opportunity through alignment, verified resume, human readiness, submission receipt, and observed outcome. At each checkpoint, answer two lightweight questions: **Is this trustworthy and fit for my purpose?** and **Did this materially improve my continuity?** No answers open contextual correction/improvement notes; trust failures create a HOLD without advancing. The demo records local dated events, supports multiple opportunities, and exports JSON/CSV for a recurring personal pilot. [Design, boundaries & review tests](./rts-checkpoint-pilot-v0-1/README.md).

This is a **publicly accessible GitHub Pages prototype** for demonstration, not an integrated RTS tracker or employment application service. It has **not** been added to the enhanced library, website showcase, or proAM Labs website. Survey answers are self-reported and browser-local; do not enter confidential data.

## What comes next (planned)

These are prospective demonstrations, not published or verified capabilities. Live demo links will be added here when the prototypes are ready.

- **Arena v0.35 — Switching Quarterbacks** *(planned)* — Add or change LLMs, note-taking apps, and other tools while preserving qualified continuity, stable contracts, provenance, and human authority.
- **POS-Lite — Mobile-First Bridge** *(in design)* — Find observations, inspect documented relationships and available metric states, ask what needs attention, and initiate a governed Output Contract from a phone.
- **Output Demo v0.4 — Intent to Artifact** *(proposed)* — Follow a small request, such as updating a demo from a phone, from prior orientation and authority checks through a bounded change, verification, and carry-forward.
