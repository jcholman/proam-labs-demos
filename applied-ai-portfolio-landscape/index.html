EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Purpose-driven and personally motivated, I am building proAM Labs as a nonprofit-oriented applied-intelligence initiative focused on Fit-for-Purpose teams of AI agents. The mission is to help individuals and organizations evaluate evidence, create shared understanding, preserve continuity of care across decisions, and move from qualified insight to human-authorized action. The demonstrations below are working expressions of that mission: practical systems designed to make evidence, uncertainty, provenance, continuity and human authority visible and useful.
