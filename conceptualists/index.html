01 · A different way of seeing

Four signals of human capability.

A proposed proAM Labs taxonomy, not an industry standard or a human scoring system. These dimensions complement one another; they are not a required sequence.

Declared · What do you say you can do?

Resumes, profiles and credentials

A claim can orient an inquiry, but does not independently verify capability.

Important: confidential or inaccessible work may still reflect substantial capability. Lack of a public portfolio is not evidence of lack of skill.

02 · Signal before action

From information to understanding—and human choice.

Observe

Identify claims, artifacts, context, and missing evidence. Recording a claim does not make it true.

Understand

Explore relationships across sources and time. Keep uncertainty, provenance, and qualification visible.

Decide

Support better-informed human choices. Capability does not equal authorization to act.

The Thinking Graph is not a score for people.

It is an exploration of how qualified understanding could survive changes in tools, work, and time. Similarity in embedding space is not proof of competence. The person retains control over what to disclose, pursue, or leave behind.
