This browser-local prototype expands your words into a small, curated concept vocabulary—such as readiness, missing assets, execution environment, and candidate alignment—then ranks the included records.

It demonstrates a semantic search experience. It does not call an embedding model, query the full corpus, or read a live Thinking Graph. Matching is an aid to discovery, not qualification or a new graph relationship.

Source and package states are dated snapshots. Opening a link requires your existing access. Nothing here updates Drive, a tracker, or the original conversation.
