03 · Interpret with care

Assumptions & limitations

What it models: the capacity value of fewer restarts, faster orientation, and less repeated context reconstruction.

People × (minutes saved ÷ 60) × workdays × (realization % ÷ 100) × fully loaded hourly cost
  • The 100 people, 15 minutes, $75/hour, 220 days, and 60% realization are illustrative defaults, not measured outcomes.
  • Actual savings depend on the current baseline, successful adoption, task mix, data quality, operational design, and what employees do with recovered time.
  • Time recovered is not automatically a reduction in payroll or a financial return.
  • Costs must include setup, integrations, maintenance, evidence qualification, human review, and change management; unspecified costs remain unknown.
  • Risk reduction, faster decisions, retention, trust, cognitive relief, and other potential benefits are excluded because no credible numeric basis is established here.
04 · From hypothesis to evidence

What proAM Labs is trying to test

The goal is not simply a better summary. It is the ability to reconstruct qualified understanding with evidence, context, uncertainty, relationships, and decision boundaries intact.

Proof-of-value question: Can another person or agent use preserved context to reach a reliable, decision-useful understanding with less effort than conventional retrieval or an ordinary LLM assistant?

  • Compare source search, LLM-assisted retrieval, and governed continuity on the same tasks.
  • Measure time to orientation, factual fidelity, recognition of unknowns, decision quality, and total cost of transfer.
  • Use independently checked evidence and observe real-world adoption before claiming benefit.

Commercial readiness: This prototype illustrates a possible value mechanism. It does not demonstrate technical effectiveness, scalable unit economics, product-market fit, or a price customers will pay.
