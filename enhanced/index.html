Continuity Search

Idea → prior thread → artifacts → continuation

Continuity v0.1Available prototype

Find Your Thread

Where did we leave off—and how do we keep going?

Search by an idea or unfinished task, inspect what was understood and where the work stopped, browse Publication or RTS artifact packages, explore the Demo tab with the calculator and Arena, and prepare an editable continuation brief. One publication thread can contain several article packages; an opportunity keeps its company-and-role identity.

Four de-identified examples demonstrate concept and synonym matching. This is a browser-local search experience, not a live corpus, embedding index, or Thinking Graph connection. Private conversation and artifact links are excluded; no sign-in is required.

Continuity Value Explorer

Assumptions → recovered capacity → evidence needed

Value v0.1Available prototype

What Could Continuity Be Worth?

If we spend less time starting over, what might that capacity be worth?

Adjust affected employees, minutes recovered per day, fully loaded labor cost, workdays, and realization. See potential annual recovered hours and capacity value, and optionally factor in implementation and operating costs.

This is an illustrative economic model—not verified Thinking Graph savings, cash savings, a customer price, or a financial forecast. Unspecified costs remain unknown; the page explains assumptions, disclaimers, and how a proof-of-value experiment could test the hypothesis.
