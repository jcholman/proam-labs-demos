### What v0.2 adds

An uncluttered, responsive **24-node constellation** appears on entry. Click any observation to bring it to the center and reveal the locally documented historical statement network, including source/target terms and explicit predicates. Other observations remain visible at the perimeter **without invented connecting lines**.

The currently retrieved governed bindings support:
- 24 independently readback-verified observations.
- 12 observation neighborhoods with statement-level details.
- 36 recorded historical TG statements in those neighborhoods.
- **No independently loaded, current canonical cross-observation edge inventory.** No such edges are rendered or implied.

The status of *the historical statement* is separated from *current canonical authority*. A solid cyan line represents a recorded historical statement. A gold dashed line represents a historically recorded `PROPOSES` or `CANDIDATE_FOR` predicate. Unresolved branch parents on OBS-027 and OBS-034 remain explicit dashed open endpoints (OBS-034 condition is carried from PO specification; its Relationship Index row has not been independently retrieved for this public projection). The surrounding 24-node arrangement is **visual placement only**, not semantic or temporal ordering.

A connection not shown means **NOT_EVIDENCED_IN_THIS_PROJECTION**, not that the records are unrelated.
