    Contract boundary

    What this demo does—and does not establish

    Operational continuity

    • Each accepted stage records a dated user-attested milestone with an optional supporting locator.
    • A negative trust evaluation creates a HOLD and invites a corrected review, without erasing earlier feedback.
    • A negative continuity evaluation is saved as a refinement candidate, not an automatic blocker.

    Governance and claims

    • Evidence reference ≠ independently verified evidence. This demo cannot inspect ATS receipts or validate claims.
    • Ready to Submit ≠ submission. Human review is required; no external application is performed.
    • Wall-clock elapsed time ≠ active work or saved time. The duration includes breaks, waiting and nights. User-corrected historical timestamps are flagged; system clock accuracy is not independently verified.
    • Self-estimated time ≠ measured benefit. Wall-clock includes overnight waits and does not represent active effort. Optional reporting does not establish causal value.
