WHAT THIS IS — AND IS NOT

RTS v0.3 is a working browser prototype and read-only presentation of the opportunity tracker. The actual resume artifacts were produced through an LLM-assisted, human-reviewed workflow and preserved in Google Drive—not automatically generated or submitted by this webpage.

Production AI deployment, external employer receipts and outcome claims require their own evidence.

Limits and candidate authority

The employer may inspect demonstrated prototype capabilities and decide their relevance. A prototype, working pilot and verified production deployment are different maturity states. No application receipt is inferred from a saved resume or a candidate-reported submission. Exact dates and independently verified employer outcomes remain evidence-dependent. The Google Sheet owns the live tracker state; this page displays a dated portfolio index for resume versions and can link to the live RTS workspace.
