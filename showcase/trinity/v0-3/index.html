Market pilot

After candidate approval, share the appropriate resume through selected channels, recording platform, exact version, date, qualified opportunity, recruiter contacts, interviews and eventual outcomes. LinkedIn and job boards serve as external channels; they do not become the source of truth. Perplexity is provisionally classified as a research/sourcing assistant rather than an employer ATS.

Privacy: This public code preview intentionally excludes the source Master's email and phone. No forms, tracking pixels, browser storage, background sync or applications are enabled. Actual personal resume files remain under governed RTS storage until specifically approved for distribution.
