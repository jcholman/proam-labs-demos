Recognition is where we notice a pattern worth exploring. Qualification is where we determine what the evidence can actually support.

01 / OBSERVE

The individual threads

Every observation carries its own time, perspective, context, and uncertainty. A thread can be preserved without being accepted as truth.

02 / RECOGNIZE

Where the fabric rises

As relationships emerge, a ridge may become visible. The experience can help someone navigate toward the signal beneath the surface.

03 / QUALIFY

Understanding with boundaries

A meaningful pattern still needs provenance, testing, and human judgment. Understanding does not automatically authorize action.
