An illustrative job-seeker need—not a testimonial or measured pilot result.

How a path takes shape

A system that starts
with understanding.

  1. Recognize what is already there

    Roles, experience, projects, skills, interests and constraints.

  2. Explore relevant support

    Programs and organizations with source-backed descriptions and honest eligibility questions.

  3. Choose the next action

    Apply, contact a counselor, explore training—or hold while something needs verification.

  4. Return without beginning again

    Carry your resume draft, decisions and open questions into the next step.
