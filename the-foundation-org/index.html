What applied AI assistance could make possible

The right opportunity may be one you didn't know to ask about.

You might come looking for help with a resume, a career change, or understanding a repayment option. There may also be a training program, housing resource, or other community service worth exploring. The aim is to connect what matters to you with possibilities you may not yet see—without assuming what you need or what you qualify for.

01Start with you

Your experience, goals, and what you choose to share.

02Find a possible connection

Explore relevant programs and the evidence behind them.

03Check before recommending

A human reviews fit, costs, unanswered questions, and the provider's rules.

04You choose what comes next

Decide whether to investigate, contact a provider, or hold.

Our current status: This is the proposed service model. Today you can build a browser-based career profile and explore a source-reviewed catalog. Personalized AI matching, benefits screening, eligibility decisions, and referrals are not operating through this website.
