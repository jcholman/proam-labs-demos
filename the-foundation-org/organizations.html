03 / Organizations and local opportunity

Find capability.
Honor the person behind it.

A stronger local workforce begins when experience is recognized. Organizations could eventually connect with people whose skills and aspirations fit real opportunities—without gaining access to their private histories.

Illustrative model only · No fees collected, employer accounts, verified candidate profiles or matching service in this demo.

A proposed two-sided model

Community value is not the same as extracting personal data.

The individual experience is designed to be accessible without charge. A future organization-facing offering could support the mission through paid services: consent-based talent introductions, workforce insights, and assisted opportunity matching.

That is different from charging companies to browse everything a job seeker has entered.

Access is earned through clear purpose, appropriate consent, review and authorization—not merely payment.

A boundary worth protecting

“The individual owns the story. The organization earns the invitation.”

Proposed governance principle for the Foundation and any future commercial service.

What an organization could see

Three visibility levels.
Only one is shared by choice.

Public resources, opt-in capability profiles, and private career evidence are separate data classes.

Public

Community resources, occupational pathways, program information and aggregated, disclosure-safe trends.

Available without a person’s private profile.

Shared by choice

Skills, target work, location preferences and an approved resume or portfolio—only within the individual’s explicit permissions.

Requires opt-in, purpose and revocation controls.

Private

Unsubmitted applications, personal notes, barriers, sensitive information, rejected paths, counselor conversations and raw reasoning history.

Not employer inventory. Not unlocked by a fee.

How this could be sustained

Free to the individual.
Funded through aligned value.

An organization-facing fee model is proposed, not launched. Terms, organizational separation, nonprofit benefit, conflict-of-interest controls, privacy, candidate protections and legal review must be resolved before any commercial access exists.
