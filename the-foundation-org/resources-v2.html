02 / Your community

Find support that fits
where you are.

Finding a program is one thing. Knowing whether it can actually help is another.

Career guidance, mentoring, training, and practical support are available through a variety of organizations. This directory gives you a place to start, with information from published sources, questions worth asking, and links to the providers themselves.

Sometimes the right opportunity is one you didn't know to look for.

But finding it is only the beginning. A program may change its requirements, run out of funding, or stop accepting participants. Information that was accurate last month may need to be checked again today.

We're exploring how AI, reliable sources, and human review could help people recognize useful opportunities, understand what's changed, and preserve what they've learned for their next decision.

For now, this is an exploratory directory—not a personalized benefits screener, eligibility determination, or live referral service.
